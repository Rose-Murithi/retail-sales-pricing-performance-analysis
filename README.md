# Retail Sales, Pricing & Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project is an Excel-based analysis of retail transaction data designed to demonstrate how Excel can be used to organize data, perform calculations, analyze sales performance, and communicate business insights.

The project uses a 50-transaction sample for the analysis while preserving the complete original dataset in the **Raw Data** sheet.

The analysis focuses on sales before and after discounts, discount activity, units sold, product categories, payment methods, sales performance, and business insights.

## Project Objectives

- Preserve the original dataset for reference
- Prepare a smaller sample for practical analysis
- Calculate sales amounts before and after discounts
- Calculate discount amounts and discount rates
- Analyze sales and units sold by product category
- Analyze monthly sales performance
- Examine the distribution of total discount value across product categories
- Analyze transaction distribution by payment method
- Apply logical conditions and Excel formulas to transaction data
- Summarize results into a business-focused report
- Identify practical patterns and insights from the data

## Workbook Structure

### 1. Raw Data

Contains the complete original retail transaction dataset, preserved separately from the analysis.

### 2. Analysis Data

Contains the 50-transaction sample used for the project, together with calculated fields for sales amounts, discounts, and amounts after discount.

### 3. Retail Sales Performance

Contains detailed analysis of the retail transactions, including sales performance, discounts, units sold, product categories, payment methods, and supporting calculations.

### 4. Business Summary

Presents the main results in a concise business-focused format, including overall performance, category shares, payment method distribution, and key business insights.

## Key Findings

The analysis of the 50 transactions produced the following results:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales Before Discount | $15,584.22 |
| Total Discounts Given | $1,958.20 |
| Total Sales After Discount | $13,626.02 |
| Discount Rate | 12.57% |
| Sales Retention After Discounts | 87.43% |
| Total Transactions | 50 |
| Total Units Sold | 260 |

### Category Performance

**Books** recorded the largest share of total sales at **38.30%** and the largest share of total units sold at **34.62%**. Books also accounted for **41.72% of the total discount value**.

**Home Décor** recorded the smallest share of total sales at **13.46%** and total units sold at **15.38%**, while accounting for **12.08% of the total discount value**.

The results show that discount activity was concentrated more heavily in categories with greater sales activity. However, this analysis does not establish that higher discounts caused higher sales.
